A curious learner building solid computer science foundations and striving toward the future of secure computing.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student currently pursuing my B.Tech at JG University, Ahmedabad. Prior to my degree, I completed my Diploma in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) at the Government Polytechnic for Girls, Ahmedabad.
